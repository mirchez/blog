I'm a software engineer from Ciudad del Este, Paraguay. My best friend José and I pushed each other into programming during college in 2024.

I broke into the industry by applying to YC startups. I didn't land the role I aimed for, but a YC founder I met along the way offered me work at his venture, and I took it. We shipped production features fast to win the first clients.

That work led me to [Brightlight Health](https://brightlight.ai), a Canadian healthcare SaaS, where I now work closely with the CEO and meet with clients while shipping across the EMR end-to-end.

Outside of work I co-founded [Talkeo](https://talkeo.ai), an AI voice tutor for Spanish and Portuguese speakers learning English. We're pre-launch and applying to Y Combinator.

## Technical contributions

— At [Brightlight Health](https://brightlight.ai), I shipped multi-timezone scheduling with drag-and-drop, billing with Stripe, PDF forms using Adobe's API with LLM autofill, video sessions with JWT auth and AI transcription, and a storage migration from Azure to AWS S3 in production.

— Co-founded [Talkeo](https://talkeo.ai), an AI voice language tutor. I built the pedagogy prompts (CEFR-aligned, code-switching policy, L1 transfer error correction), the LiveKit voice pipeline, and the Fish Audio TTS integration.

— Built [Telos](https://telos-steel.vercel.app), an AI website builder that generates full sites from prompts. Next.js, tRPC, Prisma, OpenAI, with async AI workflows orchestrated through Inngest.
