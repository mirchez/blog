I'm a software engineer from Ciudad del Este, Paraguay. My best friend José and I pushed each other into programming during college in 2024.

I broke into the industry by applying to YC startups. I didn't land the role I aimed for, but a YC founder I met along the way offered me work at his venture, and I took it. We shipped production features fast to close clients in the pipeline.

That work led me to [Brightlight Health](https://www.brightlight.health), a Canadian healthcare SaaS, where I now work closely with the CEO and meet with clients while shipping across the EMR end-to-end.

Outside of work I co-founded [Talkeo](https://www.talkeo.ai), an AI voice tutor for Spanish and Portuguese speakers learning English. We're pre-launch and applying to Y Combinator.

## Technical contributions

— Software engineer at [Brightlight Health](https://www.brightlight.health), a Canadian healthcare SaaS. I ship clinical EMR features in production — scheduling, billing, video, PDF forms with LLM autofill — used by real clinics.
