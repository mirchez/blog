## What this exercises

The pipeline needs to render typography, links, code, and lists correctly
before we commit to writing real content. If something looks off here, it
will look off everywhere.

A paragraph with an [external link](https://rauchg.com) and an inline
`code snippet` should render naturally. Internal links like
[the home page](/) should also work without a full reload.
